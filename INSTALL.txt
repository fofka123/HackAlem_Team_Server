HackAlem Automation Pack v1

Copy/overwrite this archive into the ROOT of HackAlem_Team_Server.
It intentionally contains only changed/new files; it does NOT contain .env and will not overwrite your secrets.

After extraction on CachyOS:
  chmod +x scripts/*.sh scripts/dev_center.py
  ./scripts/install-dev-center.sh
  ./scripts/start-dev.sh

Then open:
  http://127.0.0.1:8766
  http://127.0.0.1:8080  -> Automation

The first dev build may take longer because backend/Dockerfile.dev installs Ruff and Pytest.
